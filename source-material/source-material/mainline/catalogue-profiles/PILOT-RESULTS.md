# Mainline catalogue cross-section pilot

The catalogue-derived candidates are available under the visualiser's
**Profile comparison → Catalogue** option. They remain experimental and do not
replace the protected current or SAM 2.1 profiles.

| SKU | Class | Points | Confidence | Result |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| POL-4875 | ornate | 38 | 0.747 | Compound Verona silhouette recovered; suitable for visual comparison |
| POL-4508 | composite | 23 | 0.672 | Black-to-silver step and sight-edge slope recovered; tuned current version remains the stronger material reference |
| POL-2104 | ornate | 45 | 0.924 | Multiple coves, beads, and outer drop recovered clearly |
| POL-4540 | reverse | 33 | 0.768 | Deep reverse channel and stepped edges recovered |
| 010-1009 | wood scoop | 27 | 0.900 | Broad scoop, flat central run, and inner bead recovered |

The important result is that the catalogue separates the finished face from the
raw substrate strongly enough for deterministic computer vision. This is much
more reliable than inferring physical geometry from Mainline's spin sequence.
It should now be tested across the remaining current Mainline SKUs, with low
confidence candidates held for review instead of silently accepted.
